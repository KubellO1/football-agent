## Technical/data contract outcome

- Named bookmakers encountered in **historical or research evidence**: Bet365, Betfair, Pinnacle and William Hill. Production-verified live named-bookmaker quotes: **0**. PinnWire and Pinnodds both attribute prices to Pinnacle and cannot count as two independent bookmakers.
- Five-league historical CSV coverage: **5/5**. Five-league fresh, approved, future-fixture 1X2 coverage: **0/5 verified**. The former must not be presented as the latter.
- T-24, T-6, T-90, T-60 and T-30 quote availability: **not verified** for any approved production source. Capturing an old Friday/Tuesday price at T-30 would create a new observation time, not a fresh source quote.
- A safe collector would need a per-source permission manifest; DB-provided future fixture IDs and due checkpoints; fixture-targeted URLs; strict competition/team/kickoff matching; full-time home/draw/away Decimal prices `>1`; explicit bookmaker identity; UTC `captured_at` separate from genuine per-quote `source_timestamp`; raw SHA-256 and parser version; append-only snapshots keyed by source/event/bookmaker/market/selection/observation identity; replay deduplication; caching, bounded requests, Retry-After, conservative rate limiting and a circuit breaker. A second later capture may be a legitimate new observation even if the odds are unchanged. No consensus/fair price may be relabeled as a bookmaker quote.
- TASK-20260920-070 previously built a research-only offline Pinnacle 1X2 normalizer on a separate unmerged branch. This task did not duplicate it or wire it into production. Its existence does not resolve source rights or live coverage.
