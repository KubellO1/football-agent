observed ledger-only database: revision `0021` remained while all 17 mapped business tables were

PostgreSQL statement logging was disabled, so this is high-confidence causal evidence rather than
a preserved statement-level record.

## Production recovery procedure prepared

The preferred method never restores over the damaged database:

1. Confirm all DB writers are disabled and no writer/Alembic sessions or locks exist.
2. Take a fresh forensic dump of current `football` and preserve/export `football-agent_pgdata`.
3. Restore the SHA-verified candidate into a clearly named staging database.
4. Run only the rehearsed 0020 → 0021 upgrade against staging.
5. Require exact schema hash, counts, sequence state, clean Alembic check, zero canonical
   duplicates, zero FK orphans and read-only application compatibility.
6. Apply no delta reconstruction without separate evidence-based approval.
7. During approved cutover, terminate application sessions, rename current `football` to a
   timestamped forensic name, then rename validated staging to `football`.
8. Revalidate before any writer resumes. Roll back by reversing the names.

Estimated cutover downtime after staging validation: **5–15 minutes**, excluding separately
approved delta reconstruction.
